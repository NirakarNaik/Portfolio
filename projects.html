HackMates

HackMates is a Tinder-style platform for developers to find the right teammates for hackathons. Swipe through developers based on skills, find complementary teammates, match, and start chatting. After the project, teammates can rate each other on communication, reliability, technical contribution, and teamwork — building a reputation system for future collaborations. Built during a 12-hour overnight hackathon, because sleep was optional.

  • Frontend: HTML,CSS,JavaSCript
  • Backend: JavaSCript/Node.js
  • Database: PostgreSQL
  • Design: Canva/Figma
  • Deployment: Vercel
